### Pre-existing drift found during the parity review (NOT introduced by this merge — open item)

`OpenAPSSMBPlugin` has a **wider** `invalidInputs` guard than `OpenAPSAIMIPlugin` / `OpenAPSAutoISFPlugin`:

| Check | SMB | AutoISF | AIMI |
|-------|-----|---------|------|
| `sens`, `carb_ratio`, `autosens.ratio` finite & > 0 | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| `variable_sens` finite & > 0 under dynISF | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| `TDD` finite & > 0 under dynISF | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| `insulinDivisor > 0` under dynISF | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| dynISF gate is `dynIsfMode && dynIsfResult.tddPartsCalculated()` | ✅ | ❌ (raw mode) | ❌ (raw mode) |
| `fabricPrivacy.logException` + `EventOpenAPSUpdateGui` on abort | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |

AIMI is currently **aligned with upstream AutoISF**, not with SMB. Structurally, AIMI's `insulinDivisor` is a
constant 45/55/65 (never ≤ 0), so only the `TDD` check and the abort observability are actually missing.
Adding the `TDD` check **must not** be done under the raw `dynIsfMode` flag — AIMI has no
`dynIsfResult.tddPartsCalculated()` equivalent, so an unconditional TDD guard could abort the loop whenever
TDD is unavailable. **Left unchanged on purpose; needs an explicit decision before touching a dosing-abort
condition.**
